\subsection{The exact physical shadow atlas}

Let
\[
\mathcal A
:=
\{(R,\sigma,\varepsilon):
0<R<\rho,\ R<\sigma<\varepsilon<E\}.
\]
For a four-parameter fibre chamber \(C\), write
\[
\pi(C)
:=
\{(R,\sigma,\varepsilon):
\exists x\ (R,x,\sigma,\varepsilon)\in C\}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[A15.1b2l-A: exact residual-shadow atlas]
\label{thm:p12-round27-atlas}
The projections of the promoted C42, C44 and Round-26 chambers are given
exactly as follows.

First,
\[
P_{42}:=\pi(C_{42})
\]
is defined by
\[
\chi-\eta<2R<\delta,
\]
\[
R<\sigma,
\qquad
R+\sigma>\chi,
\qquad
2\sigma>\delta,
\]
\[
\sigma+\varepsilon>\kappa,
\]
\[
2\sigma<3\eta+\chi,
\qquad
2\varepsilon>3\eta+\chi,
\qquad
\varepsilon<E.
\]
Second,
\[
P_{44}:=\pi(C_{44})
\]
is defined by
\[
\chi-\eta<2R<\delta,
\]
\[
3\eta+\chi<2\sigma<3\delta,
\]
\[
2\varepsilon>3\delta,
\qquad
\varepsilon<E.
\]
Finally,
\[
\pi(C_{26}^{-})=\pi(C_{26}^{+})=:P_{26},
\]
and the common projection is defined by

\]
\[

\qquad

\]

R+\sigma>2\chi-\eta,
\qquad

\qquad

If
\[
\mathcal G_{27}
:=
\mathcal A\setminus
\left(
\overline{P_{42}}^{\mathcal A}
\cup
\overline{P_{44}}^{\mathcal A}
\cup
\overline{P_{26}}^{\mathcal A}
\right),
\]
then \(\mathcal G_{27}\) has exactly one open path-connected component.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Single-variable Fourier--Motzkin elimination of \(x\) gives the displayed
systems. The retained exact verifier checks the compact descriptions against
all generated inequalities on the rationally certified polyhedral closures;
in particular the C42 system includes the indispensable wall
\(2\sigma>\delta\). It also gives identical elimination systems for
\(C_{26}^{-}\) and \(C_{26}^{+}\).

For the component statement, every relative chamber closure requires
\(R\ge r_*\). For fixed \((R,\sigma)\), chamber membership is upward-monotone
in \(\varepsilon\), apart from the common ceiling \(E\). Hence a point outside
the three relative closures can first be moved downward in \(\varepsilon\)
through a safe staircase below the relevant levels \(2\eta,s_*,t_*\), and
then to \(R<r_*\). In the latter region all three closures are absent, while
\[
0<R<r_*,\qquad R<\sigma<\varepsilon<E
\]
is convex. This supplies a common path anchor. Points on a staircase
hyperplane are handled by an arbitrarily small perturbation inside the open
set. The independent review reproduced both the elimination and the path
certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Projection firewall]
Theorem~\ref{thm:p12-round27-atlas} is a theorem about the physical
\emph{shadow} of local fibre certificates. Membership in \(P_{42}\),
\(P_{44}\) or \(P_{26}\) asserts existence of at least one \(x\) at which the
corresponding local certificate applies. It does not assert full fibre
coverage and therefore does not imply kernel triviality at that physical
parameter point.
\end{remark}
