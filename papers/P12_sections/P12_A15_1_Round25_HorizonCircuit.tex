\begin{proposition}[A15.1b2j: exact local horizon-wall circuit]
\label{prop:p12-r25-horizon}
Assume
\[
0.0195<R<0.0205,
\]
\[
0.0395<\sigma<0.0405,\qquad
0.0550<\varepsilon<0.0559.
\]
For almost every
\[
x\in I_-:=(0.0275,0.0285),
\]
every kernel vector satisfies
\[
\boxed{h(x)=0,\qquad h(\delta-x)=0.}
\]
Equivalently, for each fixed admissible \(R,\sigma,\varepsilon\), the kernel
vector vanishes on both open intervals
\[
I_-=(0.0275,0.0285),\qquad
I_+:=\delta-I_-.
\]
The \(J\)-mirror of this chamber is the corresponding local chamber on the
opposite horizon wall and has the same raw matrix coefficient-for-coefficient.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
In the minus-side chamber one has uniformly
\[
\kappa-x>\varepsilon,\qquad
x+\eta<\varepsilon.
\]
Thus the Round-23 source \((-1,5,1)\) lies above the source horizon, while
\((1,5,0)\) remains horizon-legal. Exactly 41 of the original 42 source rows
survive, acting on the original 42 visibility variables.

Add the 51-source circuit recorded in the Round-25 audit. Direct reconstruction
of every row from
\[
Lh(u)=
p[h(u-a)-h(u+a)]
+r[h(u-b)-h(u+b)]
+q[h(u-T)-h(u+T)]
\]
using only odd reflection, the support cutoff and the horizon cutoff introduces
exactly 50 additional visibility coordinates. Hence the 41 old rows and 51
circuit rows form a square raw matrix
\[
\boxed{M_{92}\in M_{92}(\mathbb R).}
\]
No reduced or hand-inserted equation is used.

Factor \(p\) from every row and put
\[
\beta=q/p=2^{-3/4},
\qquad
\alpha=r/p
=\sqrt{\frac{\log3}{\log2}}\left(\frac23\right)^{3/4}.
\]
Then
\[
\det M_{92}=p^{92}D_{92}(\beta,\alpha).
\]
Directed-rounding interval Gaussian elimination gives the rigorous enclosure
\[
D_{92}\in
(
1.9850792121557575604061864810750\times10^{-5},
1.9850792121557575604139727620295\times10^{-5}
).
\]
Therefore
\[
\det M_{92}\ne0.
\]
All 92 live coordinates vanish, including \(h(x)\) and
\(h(\delta-x)\).

The independent Round-25 GREEN review reimplemented the raw-matrix build,
the directed interval elimination and the involution check, reproducing the
same positive determinant enclosure and confirming
\[
M_{\rm mirror}=M_{92}.
\]
Thus the same certificate holds in the \(J\)-reflected plus-horizon chamber.
\end{proof}

\end{remark}
