\section{Round-28 central next-shell double-horizon box}
\label{sec:p12-round28-double-horizon}

Retain
\[
\eta=e-2\delta,\qquad
\chi=3\delta-e,\qquad
\kappa=e-\delta,
\qquad
E=\varepsilon_{\max}.
\]

Define the open \(J\)-symmetric box \(B_{28}\) by
\[
\frac{19}{2000}<R<\frac{21}{2000},
\qquad
\left|x-\frac{\delta}{2}\right|<\frac1{5000},
\]
\[
\frac{119}{2000}<\sigma<\frac{121}{2000},
\qquad
\frac{139}{2000}<\varepsilon<\frac{141}{2000}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[A15.1b2m: central next-shell double-horizon certificate]
\label{thm:p12-round28-m68}
For every parameter point in \(B_{28}\), every kernel vector of the P12
three-shift raw operator satisfies
\[
\boxed{h(x)=h(\delta-x)=0}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Directed rational bounds for \(\log2,\log3,\log5\) give, throughout
\(B_{28}\),
\[
0<R<\rho,\qquad R<x,\qquad R<\sigma<\varepsilon<E.
\]
Moreover
\[
\sigma+x>\kappa,\qquad \sigma-x>\eta,
\]
so both first support-shell variables
\[
U_-=(-1,5,1),\qquad U_+=(1,5,0)
\]
are live.  At the same time
\[
\varepsilon+x<2\delta,\qquad
\varepsilon-x<\delta,
\]
so the two natural Round-24 next-shell sources
\[
V_-=(-1,4,4),\qquad V_+=(1,4,3)
\]
are both above the source horizon.

Consequently the old 42-source block has 42 legal rows and 44 visible
variables.  Add the following 26 sources:
\[
\begin{aligned}
&(-1,-1,3),(-1,0,3),(-1,0,4),(-1,1,-1),(-1,1,4),\\
&(-1,2,-2),(-1,2,-1),(-1,2,5),(-1,3,-1),(-1,3,5),\\
&(-1,3,6),(-1,4,-1),(-1,5,0),\\
&(1,-1,2),(1,0,2),(1,0,3),(1,1,-2),(1,1,3),\\
&(1,2,-3),(1,2,-2),(1,2,4),(1,3,-2),(1,3,4),\\
&(1,3,5),(1,4,-2),(1,5,-1).
\end{aligned}
\]
Together with the 42 old sources these form 68 distinct sources, closed
under
\[
J(s,m,n)=(-s,m,n+s).
\]
Their raw rows generate exactly 68 visible variables, again \(J\)-closed
and containing the coordinates of \(h(x)\) and \(h(\delta-x)\).  Hence the
coefficient matrix is a square matrix \(M_{68}\).

The complete source/sign/support/horizon inventory contains 1204 strict
affine inequalities.  Directed rational interval evaluation over the whole
box proves all 1204 inequalities strictly, with minimum certified raw margin
greater than
\[
0.00157927617.
\]
Thus the symbolic matrix is constant on all of \(B_{28}\).

In natural \(J\)-ordering,
\[
M_{68}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
A_{34}&B_{34}\\
B_{34}&A_{34}
\end{pmatrix},
\]
and therefore
\[
\det M_{68}
=
\det(A_{34}+B_{34})\det(A_{34}-B_{34}).
\]
Set
\[
\beta=\frac qp=2^{-3/4},
\qquad
v=\left(\frac rp\right)^2
=\frac{\log3}{\log2}\sqrt{\frac8{27}}.
\]
The two parity determinants factor exactly as
\[
\det(A_{34}+B_{34})
=
-p^{12}qr^8F_+(p,q,r),
\]
\[
\det(A_{34}-B_{34})
=
p^{12}qr^8F_-(p,q,r),
\]
where \(F_\pm\) are homogeneous of degree 13 and
\[
F_-(p,q,r)=F_+(p,-q,r).
\]
Writing
\[
F_+(p,q,r)=p^{13}G_+(\beta,v),
\qquad
F_-(p,q,r)=p^{13}G_-(\beta,v),
\]
the independently reproduced rigorous enclosures are
\[
0.3822065426030501
<
G_+(\beta,v)
<
0.38220654260305015
\]
and
\[
0.20288790549604774
<
G_-(\beta,v)
<
0.20288790549604777.
\]
Hence
\[
\det M_{68}
=
-p^{68}\beta^2v^8G_+(\beta,v)G_-(\beta,v)
\ne0.
\]
The 68 visible variables therefore vanish, in particular
\(h(x)=h(\delta-x)=0\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Round-28 scope]
\label{rem:p12-round28-scope}
This theorem closes only the exact box \(B_{28}\).  It does not close the
whole next-shell horizon gap, the two cells in which exactly one of
\(V_-,V_+\) is horizon-legal, the deeper horizon remainder, or the
outer-core region.  No new global radius threshold follows.  The size 68 is
the size of this retained certificate; no minimality or canonical meaning is
claimed.
\end{remark}
