\section{Round-29 epsilon bridge through the next-shell horizon walls}
\label{sec:p12-round29-epsilon-bridge}

Retain
\[
\eta=e-2\delta,\qquad
\chi=3\delta-e,\qquad
\kappa=e-\delta,
\qquad E=\varepsilon_{\max},
\]
and write
\[
x=\frac\delta2+y.
\]
Define the open \(J\)-symmetric box \(B_{29}\) by
\[
\frac{19}{2000}<R<\frac{21}{2000},
\qquad
|y|<\frac1{5000},
\]
\[
\frac{119}{2000}<\sigma<\frac{121}{2000},
\qquad
\frac{139}{2000}<\varepsilon<\frac{11}{100}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[A15.1b2n: epsilon-invariant M68 corridor]
\label{thm:p12-round29-epsilon-bridge}
For every parameter point in \(B_{29}\), every kernel vector of the P12
three-shift raw operator satisfies
\[
\boxed{h(x)=h(\delta-x)=0}.
\]
Moreover, \(B_{29}\) contains open regions in all four horizon orientations
for the two natural next-shell sources \(V_-\) and \(V_+\), and an upper
open slab of \(B_{29}\) lies in the already certified C44 chamber.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Directed rational bounds for \(\log2,\log3,\log5\) give throughout
\(B_{29}\)
\[
0<R<\rho,\qquad R<x,\qquad R<\sigma<\varepsilon<E,
\]
and
\[
\sigma+x>\kappa,\qquad
\sigma-x>\eta,
\qquad
\sigma>2\eta.
\]
Thus both first support-shell variables
\[
U_-=(-1,5,1),\qquad U_+=(1,5,0)
\]
remain live on the whole box.

Set
\[
\Phi_-:=\varepsilon+x-2\delta,
\qquad
\Phi_+:=\varepsilon-x-\delta.
\]
Then \(V_-=(-1,4,4)\) is horizon-legal exactly when \(\Phi_->0\), while
\(V_+=(1,4,3)\) is horizon-legal exactly when \(\Phi_+>0\).
The lower slice
\[
\frac{139}{2000}<\varepsilon<\frac{141}{2000}
\]
has
\[
\Phi_-<0,\qquad \Phi_+<0,
\]
so it is the Round-28 double-illegal side.  The open box
\[
\frac1{10000}<y<\frac1{5000},
\qquad
\frac{883}{10000}<\varepsilon<\frac{221}{2500}
\]
has
\[
\Phi_->0,\qquad \Phi_+<0,
\]
and its exact \(J\)-mirror has
\[
\Phi_-<0,\qquad \Phi_+>0.
\]
Finally, the entire slab
\[
\frac{181}{2000}<\varepsilon<\frac1{10}
\]
over the same \((R,y,\sigma)\)-base satisfies the full C44 inequalities
\[
R<x<\delta-R,
\qquad
\chi-R<x<\eta+R,
\]
\[
\max\{\kappa-x,x+\eta\}<\sigma
<\min\{2\delta-x,x+\delta\},
\]
\[
\max\{2\delta-x,x+\delta\}<\varepsilon<E.
\]
Thus \(B_{29}\) crosses both single-\(V\) orientations and reaches an open
C44 overlap.

It remains to show that the Round-28 certificate survives these horizon
crossings.  Let \(S_{68}\) be exactly the 68-source set used in
Theorem~\ref{thm:p12-round28-m68}.  Crucially,
\[
V_-,V_+\notin S_{68}.
\]
For a fixed source in \(S_{68}\), the visibility of each of its six shifted
slots depends on \((R,x,\sigma)\), not on \(\varepsilon\).  Within the
retained raw certificate, \(\varepsilon\) occurs only in the 68 source-upper
conditions
\[
T+\varepsilon-u_s>0,
\qquad s\in S_{68},
\]
and the coefficient of \(\varepsilon\) in every one of these inequalities
is exactly \(+1\).  The remaining 1136 raw conditions are
\(\varepsilon\)-independent.  In particular neither
\[
\varepsilon+x-2\delta
\qquad\text{nor}\qquad
\varepsilon-x-\delta
\]
is a raw wall of the selected \(M_{68}\)-certificate.

The complete source/sign/support/horizon inventory therefore again contains
exactly 1204 strict affine inequalities.  Directed rational interval
evaluation over all of \(B_{29}\) proves every one strictly, with minimum
certified raw margin greater than
\[
0.00157927617.
\]
Hence the selected source set, visible-variable set, row/column ordering and
every symbolic coefficient are constant throughout \(B_{29}\).

At the Round-28 reference point
\[
(R,x,\sigma,\varepsilon)
=
\left(0.010,\frac\delta2,0.060,0.070\right)
\]
this matrix is exactly the promoted Round-28 block \(M_{68}\).  Independent
review additionally reconstructed representative points in all four horizon
orientations and found coefficient-for-coefficient the same symbolic matrix.
Consequently, on all of \(B_{29}\),
\[
M_{68}^{(29)}=M_{68}^{(28)}.
\]
By Theorem~\ref{thm:p12-round28-m68},
\[
\det M_{68}^{(28)}\ne0,
\]
so no new determinant computation is required and
\[
\det M_{68}^{(29)}\ne0.
\]
All 68 visible variables vanish, in particular
\(h(x)=h(\delta-x)=0\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Round-29 scope]
\label{rem:p12-round29-scope}
This theorem closes only the exact epsilon-corridor \(B_{29}\).  It does not
close all single-\(V\) cells for arbitrary \((R,x,\sigma)\), the entire
next-shell horizon front, the deeper horizon remainder, or the outer-core
region.  No new global radius threshold follows, and no minimality or
canonical significance of the 68-row certificate is asserted.
\end{remark}
