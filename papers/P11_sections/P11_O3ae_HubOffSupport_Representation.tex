\subsection{Antisymmetry and hub off-support formula; annular Schur rewriting}
\label{subsec:o3ae-hub-off-support}

R31 reduces the R30-F question to the concrete annular cancellation
\[
 \Delta_{R,S}^{[T_0]}\ne0\qquad\text{for every }0<R<S<T_0,
\]
and shows via the R31-D countermodel that coarse Schur properties are
insufficient.  The present subsection isolates two exact structural facts of
the source hub \(H_{T_0}\) itself and records how they rewrite the annular
Schur term.  It does \emph{not} claim a kernel representation of the finite
horizon Schur term \(\Sigma_{T_0}=H_{T_0}B_{T_0}H_{T_0}^*\), because the
inverse \(B_{T_0}=(I+R_{T_0}^*R_{T_0})^{-1}\) is not treated here.  In
particular this module does not decide R30-F.  No polar-gauge,
terminal-transport, Object-X, or RH conclusion is drawn.

\subsubsection*{Notation}

For \((p,k)\) with \(p\) prime and \(k\ge1\), abbreviate
\[
 \tau_{p,k}:=\frac{k\log p}{2},\qquad
 c_{p,k}:=\sqrt{\log p}\,p^{-3k/4}.
\]
The active fixed-horizon index set is
\[
 \mathcal P_{T_0}:=\{(p,k):p\text{ prime},\ k\ge1,\ p^k\le e^{2T_0}\},
\]
which is finite.

\begin{proposition}[Antisymmetry of the source hub]
\label{prop:o3ae-antisymmetry}
On \(L^2(-T_0,T_0)\) the source hub \(H_{T_0}\) is bounded and antisymmetric:
\[
 \boxed{H_{T_0}^*=-H_{T_0}.}
\tag{O3AE.1}\label{eq:o3ae-antisymmetry}
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By \eqref{eq:hub-operator},
\(H_{T_0}=P_{T_0}\!\!\sum_{(p,k)\in\mathcal P_{T_0}}\!\!c_{p,k}D_{k\log p}E_{T_0}\)
with \(P_{T_0}=E_{T_0}^*\) and
\(D_s=U_{s/2}-U_{-s/2}\).  Translations on \(L^2(\mathbb R)\) satisfy
\(U_a^*=U_{-a}\), hence
\(D_s^*=U_{-s/2}-U_{s/2}=-D_s\).  Linearity gives
\(M^*=-M\) for
\(M:=\sum_{(p,k)\in\mathcal P_{T_0}}c_{p,k}D_{k\log p}\) on \(L^2(\mathbb R)\).
Adjunction on nested \(L^2\)-spaces gives
\[
 H_{T_0}^*
 =(P_{T_0}ME_{T_0})^*
 =E_{T_0}^*M^*P_{T_0}^*
 =P_{T_0}(-M)E_{T_0}
 =-H_{T_0}.
\]
The finiteness of \(\mathcal P_{T_0}\) and boundedness of \(H_{T_0}\) are from
Section~\ref{sec:source}.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact off-support formula for the source hub]
\label{thm:o3ae-hub-off-support}
Fix \(0<R<T_0\).  Let \(f\in L^2(-T_0,T_0)\) with
\(\operatorname{ess\,supp}(f)\subset[-R,R]\).  Then for a.e. \(u\in(-T_0,-R)\cup(R,T_0)\),
\[
 \boxed{
 (H_{T_0}f)(u)
 =\operatorname{sgn}(u)\!\!\sum_{\substack{(p,k)\in\mathcal P_{T_0}\\
   |u|-R\le\tau_{p,k}\le|u|+R}}\!\!\!\!c_{p,k}\,f\bigl(u-\operatorname{sgn}(u)\tau_{p,k}\bigr).
 }
\tag{O3AE.2}\label{eq:o3ae-hub-off-support}
\]
In particular, on each connected off-support component \(H_{T_0}f\) is a finite
signed sum of translated copies of \(f\), with translation amounts drawn from
the finite set \(\{\tau_{p,k}:(p,k)\in\mathcal P_{T_0}\}\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For \(u>R\), \(u+\tau_{p,k}>R\), so \(f(u+\tau_{p,k})=0\).  Also
\(f(u-\tau_{p,k})\ne0\) only if \(u-\tau_{p,k}\in(-R,R)\), i.e.
\(\tau_{p,k}\in(u-R,u+R)\).  Since \(u<T_0\),
\(u\in(-T_0,T_0)\), so \(P_{T_0}\) acts as the identity at this point.
Summation over \(\mathcal P_{T_0}\) gives
\[
 (H_{T_0}f)(u)
 =\sum_{(p,k):\,\tau_{p,k}\in(u-R,u+R)}c_{p,k}\,f(u-\tau_{p,k}).
\]
For \(u<-R\), \(u-\tau_{p,k}<-R\) forces
\(f(u-\tau_{p,k})=0\); a symmetric argument gives the sgn-adjusted formula.  The
\(\operatorname{sgn}(u)\)-collected form is~\eqref{eq:o3ae-hub-off-support}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Scope of Theorem~\ref{thm:o3ae-hub-off-support}]
\label{rem:o3ae-scope}
Formula~\eqref{eq:o3ae-hub-off-support} is an exact off-support formula for
\(H_{T_0}\) alone.  It requires no regularity of \(f\) beyond \(L^2\) with
compact support in \([-R,R]\), and no assumption on the archimedean symbol
\(m_\Gamma\).  It does not extend directly to \(\Sigma_{T_0}\); see the
Firewall below.
\end{remark}

\subsubsection*{Annular rewriting of the Schur term under antisymmetry}

Recall
\[
 \Sigma_{T_0}=H_{T_0}B_{T_0}H_{T_0}^*,\qquad
 B_{T_0}=(I+R_{T_0}^*R_{T_0})^{-1}.
\]

\begin{corollary}[Antisymmetric rewriting of the Schur term on the off-support]
\label{cor:o3ae-sigma-rewriting}
Let \(f\in L^2(-T_0,T_0)\) with \(\operatorname{ess\,supp}(f)\subset[-R,R]\).
Set
\[
 g:=B_{T_0}H_{T_0}^*f
 =-B_{T_0}H_{T_0}f\in L^2(-T_0,T_0).
\]
Then \(g\in L^2(-T_0,T_0)\), and for a.e.
\(u\in(-T_0,-R)\cup(R,T_0)\),
\[
 \boxed{
 (\Sigma_{T_0}f)(u)
 =\sum_{(p,k)\in\mathcal P_{T_0}}c_{p,k}
 \bigl[g(u-\tau_{p,k})-g(u+\tau_{p,k})\bigr].
 }
\tag{O3AE.3}\label{eq:o3ae-sigma-off-support}
\]
Here \(g\) is understood by zero extension outside \((-T_0,T_0)\).  This is an
identity, not a kernel representation of \(\Sigma_{T_0}\); the auxiliary function
\(g\) is defined implicitly through the bounded but nonlocal inverse \(B_{T_0}\), and
no off-support structure for \(g\) is claimed here.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Proposition~\ref{prop:o3ae-antisymmetry} and boundedness of \(B_{T_0}\) give
\(g\in L^2(-T_0,T_0)\) and \(\Sigma_{T_0}f=H_{T_0}g\).  Now use the defining formula
\[
 H_{T_0}=P_{T_0}\sum_{(p,k)\in\mathcal P_{T_0}}
 c_{p,k}D_{k\log p}E_{T_0}
\]
directly.  For a.e. \(u\in(-T_0,T_0)\), \(P_{T_0}\) is the identity and
\(D_{k\log p}E_{T_0}g(u)=g(u-\tau_{p,k})-g(u+\tau_{p,k})\), with the stated zero-extension convention.  Summing gives~\eqref{eq:o3ae-sigma-off-support}.  The support-restricted Theorem~\ref{thm:o3ae-hub-off-support} is not invoked for \(g\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[What Corollary~\ref{cor:o3ae-sigma-rewriting} is and is not]
\label{rem:o3ae-scope-sigma}
Identity~\eqref{eq:o3ae-sigma-off-support} \emph{rewrites} the annular Schur
value in terms of a fixed finite \(\{\tau_{p,k}\}\)-translation sum of the
auxiliary vector \(g=B_{T_0}H_{T_0}^*f\).  It is
\emph{not} a kernel representation of \(\Sigma_{T_0}\), it is
\emph{not} a range statement, and it does \emph{not} provide any off-support
structural constraint on \(\Sigma_{T_0}f\) beyond what an \(L^2\) shift sum
already gives.  In particular Corollary~\ref{cor:o3ae-sigma-rewriting} on its
own does not rule out any of the coarse abstract Schur models allowed by the
R31-D countermodel; it displays the structural gate but does not close it.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The annular gate under antisymmetric rewriting]
\label{rem:o3ae-gap}
Combine~\eqref{eq:o3ae-sigma-off-support} with the R31 annular identity
\eqref{eq:o3ad-forced-cancellation}.  The P02-to-P11 symbol bridge required there is
proved below in Corollary~\ref{cor:o3af-symbol-bridge}.  For the inherited vector
\(j_{R,S}=E_{R,S}\rho_{R,T_0}\) and
\[
 g_{R,S}:=-B_{T_0}H_{T_0}E_{S,T_0}j_{R,S},
\]
the identity
\[
 \sum_{(p,k)\in\mathcal P_{T_0}}c_{p,k}
 \bigl[g_{R,S}(u-\tau_{p,k})-g_{R,S}(u+\tau_{p,k})\bigr]
 =\phi_S(u)-(C_{\Gamma,S}j_{R,S})(u)
\tag{O3AE.4}\label{eq:o3ae-annular-gate}
\]
holds a.e. on the \emph{entire} annulus \(\mathcal A_{R,S}\) if and only if the
annular residual \(\Delta_{R,S}^{[T_0]}\) vanishes identically, equivalently if and only
if \(s_{R,S,T_0}=0\).  Thus, for each fixed triple, disproving
\eqref{eq:o3ae-annular-gate} on even one positive-measure open subinterval is sufficient
to prove \(\Delta_{R,S}^{[T_0]}\ne0\).  Conversely, a coincidence on only one
subinterval does \emph{not} refute R30-F; the residual may still be nonzero elsewhere.

The right-hand side of~\eqref{eq:o3ae-annular-gate} is real-analytic on each half-annulus
by R31 together with the symbol bridge, whereas the left-hand side is a finite
\(\{\tau_{p,k}\}\)-shift sum of the single \(L^2\) vector \(g_{R,S}\).  Whether the
full-annulus identity can occur remains open.
\end{remark}

\begin{openproblem}[R32-F: annular gate for R30-F]
\label{open:o3ae-analytic-gap}
For each fixed \(0<R<S<T_0\), decide whether
\eqref{eq:o3ae-annular-gate} can hold a.e. on the whole annulus
\(\mathcal A_{R,S}\).  Proving that it cannot hold for every strict triple is exactly a
proof of R30-F through the annular criterion.  Producing one triple for which it does
hold on the whole annulus would refute R30-F for that triple.  Two candidate directions
remain:

(i) A cancellation-stable regularity or singularity fingerprint for the finite shift
sum of \(g_{R,S}\).  Merely proving that \(g_{R,S}\) itself is non-real-analytic is
\emph{not} sufficient: nonanalytic contributions from distinct translates may cancel in
a finite linear combination.  One would need a property (for example an appropriate
singular-support, wavefront, or continuation invariant) that survives the prescribed
finite shift combination and is incompatible with the analytic right-hand side.
Regularity of the nonlocal inverse \(B_{T_0}\) is presently not controlled strongly
enough in the repository to provide such a fingerprint.

(ii) Fingerprint properties of the concrete annular range.  Identify a property of the
annular restriction
\(E_{S,T_0}^*H_{T_0}B_{T_0}H_{T_0}^*E_{S,T_0}\) that is
\emph{not shared} by the abstract R31-D countermodel and that is inconsistent with the
annular value \(\phi_S-C_{\Gamma,S}j_{R,S}\) whenever
\(\operatorname{supp}(j_{R,S})\subset[-R,R]\).  Positivity, boundedness, parity and
abstract positive Feshbach factorisation are covered by R31-D; the antisymmetry of
\(H_{T_0}\) alone does not by itself suffice.

Neither direction is decided by material currently in the repository; both are strict
\(?[O]\).
\end{openproblem}

\begin{remark}[Firewall]
\label{rem:o3ae-firewall}
Proposition~\ref{prop:o3ae-antisymmetry} and Theorem~\ref{thm:o3ae-hub-off-support}
are exact structural facts about \(H_{T_0}\) itself.  Corollary~\ref{cor:o3ae-sigma-rewriting}
is an \emph{annular rewriting} of \(\Sigma_{T_0}\) under antisymmetry, not a kernel
representation.  It does not by itself prove R30-F, and it does not rule out the R31-D
countermodel---the antisymmetric structure of \(H_{T_0}\) alone, without control of
\(B_{T_0}\), does not exclude the abstract Feshbach models allowed there.  The R14
firewall remains unchanged: even a future positive solution of R32-F would prove R30-F,
and R30-F itself is an inverse-functional-calculus statement only.  It would not decide
the relative polar gauge, strong terminal transport, Object~X, or RH.  The P02-to-P11
symbol bridge required in R31 is closed below by Corollary~\ref{cor:o3af-symbol-bridge};
R30-F and R32-F remain open.
\end{remark}
