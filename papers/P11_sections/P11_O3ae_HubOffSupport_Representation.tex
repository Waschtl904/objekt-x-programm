\subsection{Discrete-translational off-support representation of the source hub}
\label{subsec:o3ae-hub-off-support}

R31 reduces the R30-F question to the concrete annular cancellation
\[
 \Delta_{R,S}^{[T_0]}\ne0\qquad\text{for every }0<R<S<T_0,
\]
and shows that coarse Schur properties are insufficient.  The present subsection
opens the concrete off-support layer by expressing the source hub as a genuine
finite discrete translation sum and by recording the exact antisymmetry
\(H_{T_0}^*=-H_{T_0}\).  No polar-gauge, terminal-transport, Object-X, or RH
conclusion is drawn here.

\subsubsection*{Notation}

For \((p,k)\) with \(p\) prime and \(k\ge1\), abbreviate
\[
 \tau_{p,k}:=\frac{k\log p}{2},\qquad
 c_{p,k}:=\sqrt{\log p}\,p^{-3k/4}.
\]
The active fixed-horizon index set is
\[
 \mathcal P_{T_0}:=\{(p,k):p\text{ prime},\ k\ge1,\ p^k\le e^{2T_0}\},
\]
which is finite.

\begin{proposition}[Antisymmetry of the source hub]
\label{prop:o3ae-antisymmetry}
On \(L^2(-T_0,T_0)\) the source hub \(H_{T_0}\) is bounded and antisymmetric:
\[
 \boxed{H_{T_0}^*=-H_{T_0}.}
\tag{O3AE.1}\label{eq:o3ae-antisymmetry}
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By \eqref{eq:hub-operator},
\(H_{T_0}=P_{T_0}\!\!\sum_{(p,k)\in\mathcal P_{T_0}}\!\!c_{p,k}D_{k\log p}E_{T_0}\)
with \(P_{T_0}=E_{T_0}^*\) and
\(D_s=U_{s/2}-U_{-s/2}\).  Translations on \(L^2(\mathbb R)\) satisfy
\(U_a^*=U_{-a}\), hence
\(D_s^*=U_{-s/2}-U_{s/2}=-D_s\).  Linearity gives
\(M^*=-M\) for
\(M:=\sum_{(p,k)\in\mathcal P_{T_0}}c_{p,k}D_{k\log p}\) on \(L^2(\mathbb R)\).
Adjunction on nested \(L^2\)-spaces gives
\[
 H_{T_0}^*
 =(P_{T_0}ME_{T_0})^*
 =E_{T_0}^*M^*P_{T_0}^*
 =P_{T_0}(-M)E_{T_0}
 =-H_{T_0}.
\]
The finiteness of \(\mathcal P_{T_0}\) and boundedness of \(H_{T_0}\) are from
Section~\ref{sec:source}.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact off-support representation of the source hub]
\label{thm:o3ae-hub-off-support}
Fix \(0<R<T_0\).  Let \(f\in L^2(-T_0,T_0)\) with
\(\operatorname{ess\,supp}(f)\subset[-R,R]\).  Then for a.e. \(u\in(-T_0,-R)\cup(R,T_0)\),
\[
 \boxed{
 (H_{T_0}f)(u)
 =\operatorname{sgn}(u)\!\!\sum_{\substack{(p,k)\in\mathcal P_{T_0}\\
   |u|-R\le\tau_{p,k}\le|u|+R}}\!\!\!\!c_{p,k}\,f\bigl(u-\operatorname{sgn}(u)\tau_{p,k}\bigr).
 }
\tag{O3AE.2}\label{eq:o3ae-hub-off-support}
\]
In particular, \(H_{T_0}f\) is on each connected off-support component a finite
signed sum of translated copies of \(f\), with translation amounts drawn from
the finite set \(\{\tau_{p,k}:(p,k)\in\mathcal P_{T_0}\}\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For \(u>R\), \(u+\tau_{p,k}>R\), so \(f(u+\tau_{p,k})=0\).  Also
\(f(u-\tau_{p,k})\ne0\) only if \(u-\tau_{p,k}\in(-R,R)\), i.e.
\(\tau_{p,k}\in(u-R,u+R)\).  Since \(u<T_0\),
\(u\in(-T_0,T_0)\), so \(P_{T_0}\) acts as the identity at this point.
Summation over \(\mathcal P_{T_0}\) gives
\[
 (H_{T_0}f)(u)
 =\sum_{(p,k):\,\tau_{p,k}\in(u-R,u+R)}c_{p,k}\,f(u-\tau_{p,k}).
\]
For \(u<-R\), \(u-\tau_{p,k}<-R\) forces
\(f(u-\tau_{p,k})=0\); a symmetric argument gives the sgn-adjusted formula.  The
\(\operatorname{sgn}(u)\)-collected form is~\eqref{eq:o3ae-hub-off-support}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Structural interpretation]
\label{rem:o3ae-structural}
Formula~\eqref{eq:o3ae-hub-off-support} is exact, not asymptotic, and holds
without any regularity hypothesis on \(f\) beyond \(L^2\) with compact support.
It exposes the archimedean/nonarchimedean asymmetry of the P11 construction:
the archimedean Gamma tail (Prop~\ref{prop:o3ad-gamma-antilocal}) has a smooth,
real-analytic off-diagonal kernel, while the prime-power hub tail is exactly
supported on a translated union of the input support.
\end{remark}

\subsubsection*{Application to the finite-horizon Schur term}

Recall
\[
 \Sigma_{T_0}=H_{T_0}B_{T_0}H_{T_0}^*,\qquad
 B_{T_0}=(I+R_{T_0}^*R_{T_0})^{-1}.
\]

\begin{corollary}[Off-support structure of the Schur term]
\label{cor:o3ae-sigma-off-support}
Let \(f\in L^2(-T_0,T_0)\) with \(\operatorname{ess\,supp}(f)\subset[-R,R]\).
Set
\[
 g:=B_{T_0}H_{T_0}^*f
 =-B_{T_0}H_{T_0}f\in L^2(-T_0,T_0).
\]
For a.e. \(u\in(-T_0,-R)\cup(R,T_0)\),
\[
 \boxed{
 (\Sigma_{T_0}f)(u)
 =\sum_{(p,k)\in\mathcal P_{T_0}}c_{p,k}
 \bigl[g(u-\tau_{p,k})-g(u+\tau_{p,k})\bigr].
 }
\tag{O3AE.3}\label{eq:o3ae-sigma-off-support}
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Prop~\ref{prop:o3ae-antisymmetry} and boundedness of \(B_{T_0}\) place
\(g\in L^2(-T_0,T_0)\).  The claim is~\eqref{eq:o3ae-hub-off-support} applied
to \(g\), noting that \(g\) itself need not have compact support in \([-R,R]\)
so the \emph{full} \(D_{k\log p}\) difference formula applies rather than the
one-sided restriction of the previous theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Precise structural gap toward R30-F]
\label{rem:o3ae-gap}
Combining~\eqref{eq:o3ae-sigma-off-support} with the R31 annular
identity~\eqref{eq:o3ad-forced-cancellation}, the concrete R30-F question
\(R_*(S,T_0)=S\) is equivalent to the following analytic question for the
inherited vector \(j_{R,S}\) and its transported partner
\(g_{R,S}:=-B_{T_0}H_{T_0}E_{S,T_0}j_{R,S}\):

For \emph{no} \(0<R<S<T_0\) can the identity
\[
 \sum_{(p,k)\in\mathcal P_{T_0}}c_{p,k}
 \bigl[g_{R,S}(u-\tau_{p,k})-g_{R,S}(u+\tau_{p,k})\bigr]
 =\phi_S(u)-(C_{\Gamma,S}j_{R,S})(u)
\tag{O3AE.4}\label{eq:o3ae-precise-gate}
\]
hold for a.e. \(u\) on each connected component of the annulus \(\mathcal A_{R,S}\).
The right-hand side is real-analytic on each half-annulus by
Prop~\ref{prop:o3ad-gamma-antilocal} and the analytic
form of \(\phi_S\).  The left-hand side is a fixed finite linear combination of
translates of a single \(L^2\) function \(g_{R,S}\).
\end{remark}

\begin{openproblem}[R32-F: analytic-vs-translation-sum compatibility]
\label{open:o3ae-analytic-gap}
For every fixed \(0<R<S<T_0\), decide whether the identity
\eqref{eq:o3ae-precise-gate} can hold on any nonempty open subinterval of
\(\mathcal A_{R,S}\).  A negative answer proves R30-F.  Two candidate strategies:

(i) Show \(g_{R,S}\) is not real-analytic near the endpoint set
\(\{\pm(R+\tau_{p,k}):(p,k)\in\mathcal P_{T_0}\}\), so that its translated
combination inherits nonremovable singularities incompatible with the
analytic right-hand side.

(ii) Show that the discrete Fourier symbol
\(\sum_{(p,k)}c_{p,k}(e^{i\tau_{p,k}\xi}-e^{-i\tau_{p,k}\xi})
=2i\sum_{(p,k)}c_{p,k}\sin(\tau_{p,k}\xi)\) has an arithmetic property
(density, algebraic independence of \(\log p\)) incompatible with matching
the analytic right-hand-side symbol.

Neither strategy is decided by material currently in the repository; both are
strict \(?[O]\).
\end{openproblem}

\begin{remark}[Firewall]
\label{rem:o3ae-firewall}
Formulas~\eqref{eq:o3ae-hub-off-support}--\eqref{eq:o3ae-sigma-off-support} are
structural.  They do not by themselves prove R30-F.  They do not decide the
relative polar gauge, strong terminal transport, Object~X, or RH; the R14
firewall remains unchanged.  What R32 delivers is the reduction of the R31
open annular cancellation problem to the precise
analytic-vs-translation-sum gate~\eqref{eq:o3ae-precise-gate} on the concrete
finite prime-power translation set \(\{\tau_{p,k}\}\).
\end{remark}
