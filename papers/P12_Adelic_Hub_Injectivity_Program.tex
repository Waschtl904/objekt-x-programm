\begin{abstract}
P11 froze the finite-horizon Feshbach--Schur candidate geometry at
\(T_0\le 2a\). P12 opens the next arithmetic-shift chamber
\[
2a<T_0<\tfrac12\log5,
\]
where the third shift \(2a\) becomes active. The manuscript proves
localized-hub kernel triviality throughout the mixed strip down to
\[
\rho=\frac12\log\frac{10}{9},
\]
and, in the restricted-tail regime \(\sigma\le R\), for every \(R>0\).

Below the global \(\rho\)-threshold P12 now contains six independently
audited local overlap mechanisms: the exact Round-23 \(42\times42\) seed,
the enlarged Round-24 fixed-42 chamber, the Round-24 \(44\times44\)
support-shell chamber, the Round-25 \(92\times92\) horizon-wall circuit,
the Round-26 exact enlargement and open gluing of the paired 92-row pattern
chambers, and the Round-27 exact \(43\times43\) one-sided support shell with
its \(J\)-mirror. Round 27 additionally gives the exact physical-shadow atlas
of the C42, C44 and C26 chambers and proves that its open uncovered shadow has
one path-connected component. No global descent below \(\rho\) is inferred
from these local certificates or from the shadow atlas.

None of P12 asserts polar-gauge, terminal-transport, Object-X or RH
consequences. The R14 firewall of P11 is preserved unchanged.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup and notation}\label{sec:p12-setup}

We work throughout with the odd folded three-shift operator on
\(L^2(R,S)\) with parameters \(0<R<S<T_0\) in the arithmetic chamber
\[
2a<T_0<c:=\tfrac12\log5,
\]
where
\[
a=\tfrac12\log2,\qquad
b=\tfrac12\log3,\qquad
T=2a=\log2.
\]
The prime-power weights are
\[
p=\sqrt{\log2}\,2^{-3/4},\qquad
r=\sqrt{\log3}\,3^{-3/4},\qquad
q=\sqrt{\log2}\,2^{-3/2}.
\]
Put
\[
d=b-a,\qquad
e=T-b,\qquad
\delta=d-e,\qquad
\varepsilon_{\max}=c-T=\frac12\log\frac54,
\]
and
\[
\rho=\varepsilon_{\max}-\delta
=\frac12\log\frac{10}{9}.
\]
In the mixed strip \(T<S<T_0\), write
\[
\sigma=S-T,\qquad
\varepsilon=T_0-T,
\qquad
0<\sigma<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_{\max}.
\]

The canonical raw operator is
\[
Lh(u)=
p[h(u-a)-h(u+a)]
+r[h(u-b)-h(u+b)]
+q[h(u-T)-h(u+T)],
\]
with odd reflection and the support/horizon cutoffs inherited from P11.

\section{Persistence up to the arithmetic boundary}\label{sec:p12-persistence}

\begin{theorem}[A15.1b0: pure-tail injectivity]\label{thm:p12-b0}
Let \(2a<T_0<c\). For every \(0<R<S<T\),
\[
\ker L_{R,S,T_0}^{\{a,b,2a\}}=\{0\}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Persistence of A14.3a in the pure-source region \(S<T=2a\).
For the source family \(u=a+x\), \(0<x<a\), the additional \(2a\)-shift
has one branch beyond the source horizon and the other branch is the same
odd-reflected support term already treated in A14.3a. Hence the repaired
A14.3a proof applies unchanged.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}\label{rem:p12-b0-scope}
Theorem~\ref{thm:p12-b0} keeps \(S<T\) strict. The exact boundary
\(S=T\) is Theorem~\ref{thm:p12-b1}.
\end{remark}

\section{Consolidated status and residual overlap}\label{sec:p12-status}

\begin{corollary}[A15.1 consolidated global injectivity]
\label{cor:p12-consolidated}
Let \(2a<T_0<c\) and \(0<R<S<T_0\). Then
\[
\ker L_{R,S,T_0}^{\{a,b,2a\}}=\{0\}
\]
in the established global strata
\[
S<T,\qquad S=T,
\]
and, in the mixed strip \(T<S<T_0\),
\[
\rho\le R<T
\]
as well as the all-radius restricted-tail sector
\[
0<R<T,\qquad \sigma\le R.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[Audited local low-radius certificates]
Below \(\rho\), the following are booked as local theorems
\(\checkmark[M]_{\rm part}\):

\item A15.1b2h: Round-24 enlarged fixed-42 chamber;

\item A15.1b2l-B: Round-27 exact \(43\times43\) one-sided support shell,
      together with its exact \(J\)-mirror and exact nondegeneracy.
\end{itemize}
In addition, A15.1b2l-A is booked as the exact projected residual-shadow
atlas: \(\pi(C_{26}^{-})=\pi(C_{26}^{+})\), the compact P42/P44/P26
projections are exact, and the open uncovered physical shadow has exactly one
path-connected component. This atlas statement is existential in the fibre
variable and is not itself a kernel-triviality theorem.

The global \(\rho\)-descent and the all-radius restricted-tail theorem
remain \(\checkmark[M]\).
\end{remark}

\begin{openproblem}[Residual overlap below \(\rho\)]
\label{open:p12-below-rho-overlap}
For
\[
2a<T_0<c,\qquad
0<R<\rho,\qquad
T<S<T_0,\qquad
R<\sigma=S-T,
\]
decide whether
\[
\ker L_{R,S,T_0}^{\{a,b,2a\}}=\{0\}.
\]
The C23, enlarged C42, C44 support-shell, Round-25 horizon-circuit,
Round-26 glued horizon-corridor and Round-27 one-sided 43-row shell chambers
are already closed locally. Round 27 also proves that, after projecting the
C42/C44/C26 chambers to physical parameter space and removing their relative
closures, the open uncovered shadow is one path-connected component. This
projection records existence of certified \(x\)-fibres only and therefore
does not close the global residual-overlap problem.

The remaining task is to cover the unresolved fibre-pattern fronts inside
that single shadow component, including next-shell horizon gaps, the deeper
horizon remainder outside the C26 corridor, and the outer-core region where
the fixed M42 geometry is unavailable.

No theorem of the form \(R\ge\omega\), no replacement global low-radius
threshold, and no significance of the search depth \(21\) is booked.
\end{openproblem}

\section{Firewall}\label{sec:p12-firewall}

None of \S\ref{sec:p12-persistence}--\ref{sec:p12-status} makes a
polar-gauge, terminal-transport, Object-X or RH statement. The R14 firewall
from P11 remains unchanged: even a complete localized-hub injectivity theorem
in the three-shift chamber would remain a modulus-layer result. Neither
polar-gauge convergence nor strong terminal transport nor a canonical
mediator for Object~X follows.
