For `X<U<V`, SW14 old-conditioning is

\[
\Delta s_{\rm cond}^{U,V}(f)
=\langle v_U,(\iota^*B_V\iota-B_U)v_U\rangle,
\qquad \iota=E_{U,V},
\qquad B_T=(I+R_T^*R_T)^{-1}.
\]

Set

\[
M=R_V\iota,
\qquad
S=R_VP_{\mathcal N},
\qquad
\Phi_S=SS^*(I+SS^*)^{-1},
\qquad
\Pi=\Pi_{U,V},
\qquad
C=(I-\Pi)M.
\]

Then

\[
\Pi M=\jmath R_U,
\]

and the exact sign operator is

\[
\boxed{
K_{U,V}^{\rm Schur}
=M^*(I+SS^*)^{-1}M-R_U^*R_U
=C^*C-M^*\Phi_SM.
}
\]

The pairwise canonical order is

\[
\iota^*B_V\iota\preceq B_U
\iff
K_{U,V}^{\rm Schur}\succeq0.
\]

For the FD23 partition route it is sufficient to prove the eventual fine-step version

\[
\exists U_*,h_*>0:\quad
U\ge U_*,\ 0<V-U<h_*
\Longrightarrow K_{U,V}^{\rm Schur}\succeq0.
\]

This sign theorem is still OPEN; even if proved, reanchor/path summability remains separate.
