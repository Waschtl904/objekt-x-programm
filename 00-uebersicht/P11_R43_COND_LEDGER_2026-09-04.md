For `X<U<V`, SW14 old-conditioning is

\[
\Delta s_{\rm cond}^{U,V}(f)
=\langle v_U,(\iota^*B_V\iota-B_U)v_U\rangle,
\qquad \iota=E_{U,V},
\qquad B_T=(I+R_T^*R_T)^{-1}.
\]

Set

\[
M=R_V\iota,\quad
S=R_VP_{\mathcal N},\quad
\Phi_S=SS^*(I+SS^*)^{-1},\quad
\Pi=\Pi_{U,V},\quad
C=(I-\Pi)M.
\]

Then

\[
\Pi M=\jmath R_U,
\qquad
K_{U,V}^{\rm Schur}
=M^*(I+SS^*)^{-1}M-R_U^*R_U
=C^*C-M^*\Phi_SM.
\]

The pairwise canonical order is

\[
\iota^*B_V\iota\preceq B_U
\iff
K_{U,V}^{\rm Schur}\succeq0.
\]

Important scope: canonical/operator Loewner positivity was always a **sufficient accelerator**, not a necessary Strong-Terminal gate. The original terminal-metric audit already gives an unconditional B-FLAGMOD metric-increment route without Loewner positivity.

## D2. Breakthrough: p=2,3 pointwise-local no-go at every late terminal

Source:

`audits/P11_R43_COND_TWO_PRIME_POINTWISE_LOCAL_NOGO_2026-09-05.md`

Frozen P11 gives, for projected marks at depth `J`,

\[
\langle q_{p,k}^{(J)},q_{p,l}^{(J)}\rangle
=p^{-(k+l)/2}\bigl(p^{\min(J,k,l)}-1\bigr).
\]

In particular

\[
\langle q_{p,1}^{(2)},q_{p,2}^{(2)}\rangle
=(p-1)p^{-3/2}>0,
\]

so the first adjacent overlap in prime sector `p` contributes

\[
|I_P|(\log p)(p-1)p^{-9/4}>0.
\]

The simple full-operator kernel firewall works for the two smallest primes `p=2,3` because

\[
a_p=\tfrac12\log p<2a_2.
\]

It is **not** mechanically extended to `p>=5`; lower-prime same-side layers can then enter and would need a new argument.

For `p\in\{2,3\}` define

\[
E_p:=\{U:2U-a_p\in\Lambda\}.
\]

If `U\notin E_p`, there exists `h_p(U)>0` such that every `0<h<h_p(U)` has an exact prime-`p` kernel witness and therefore

\[
K_{U,U+h}^{\rm Schur}\not\succeq0.
\]

Moreover

\[
E_2\cap E_3=\varnothing,
\]

because simultaneous membership would imply

\[
2q^n=3r^m,\qquad n,m\ge2,
\]

contradicting unique prime factorization.

Hence the strengthened theorem is

\[
\boxed{
\exists U_0>0\ \forall U\ge U_0\ \exists h_0(U)>0\ \forall h\in(0,h_0(U)):
K_{U,U+h}^{\rm Schur}\not\succeq0.
}
\]

Further, disjointness of the integer-valued exceptional sets yields a nonoptimal absolute constant `c_*>0` such that for all sufficiently large `U`

\[
\boxed{h_0(U)\ge c_*e^{-4U}.}
\]

Therefore any actually PSD canonical step must satisfy the necessary floor

\[
\boxed{
K_{U,U+h}^{\rm Schur}\succeq0
\Longrightarrow h\ge c_*e^{-4U}.
}
\]
