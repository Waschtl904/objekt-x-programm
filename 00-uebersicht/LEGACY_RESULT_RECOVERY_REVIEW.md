## Method and evidence boundary

The primary files for C01--C20 were read from main and their current Git blob
identities were checked against the candidate inventory. For the R42/R43
candidates, the final review packages and their final-blob tables were also
read. Historical status words such as FROZEN, GREEN, READY, or FINAL were not
treated as mathematical truth by themselves.

The decisive tests were:

1. exact final blob binding;
2. explicit mathematical scope and quantifiers;
3. distinction between theorem, conditional statement, method, diagnostic, and
   open remainder;
4. later concrete objections versus mere stale status carry-forward;
5. dependency and supersession boundaries;
6. separation of fixed-pair transport from uniform, operator-norm, cofinal, or
   unbounded-horizon transport.
