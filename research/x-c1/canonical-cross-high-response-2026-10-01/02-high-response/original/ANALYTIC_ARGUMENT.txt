Stage 2: complete physical residual by direct integration
--------------------------------------------------------
For finite odd polynomials u,w, let

 r_model=u-(Qraw_model-z)w=b_cell-Vw,
 b_cell=u-(D_H+q0-z)w+K_model w+S w.

On every exact shift cell, b_cell is a polynomial. Thus the ENTIRE raw
norm is evaluated analytically from

Polynomial antiderivatives, exact logarithmic primitives for V, and the
existing exact V^2 moments account for all high degrees. No finite tail
of Legendre coefficients replaces this integral. By oddness the norm
is the corresponding integral over the positive half interval.
The polynomial/logarithmic cross terms are evaluated in each cell's local
coordinate s=(x-l)/(r-l), keeping affine arguments inside the original
interval. The local logarithmic moments use

 T_n(c)=integral_0^1 s^n/(1-c s) ds,
 T_(n-1)=1/n+c T_n,
 integral_0^1 s^n log(1-c s) ds=[log(1-c)+c T_(n+1)]/(n+1).

The top T moment is initialized by 2048 power-series terms plus the
geometric remainder |c|^2048/(1-|c|); the backward recurrence is stable.
At the singular endpoint c=1 the exact harmonic-number log moments are
used. These are directed analytic identities, not sampled quadrature.

Remove the irrelevant Mellin normal before bounding the physical norm.
Let r1=<e_1,r_model> and n=m/<e_1,m>. Then P_mperp(r_model-r1 n)=P_mperp r_model.
Let n81 be its odd Taylor polynomial through degree 81. With c=A/2,

 ||n-n81|| <= [c^83/(83! (1-c^2/(84*85)))]/<e_1,m> = epsilon_n.

Since projection contracts,

The first norm is integrated exactly in the same complete manner above.
The remaining model-to-true error is gamma_K ||w||, where gamma_K is
the published uniform Gamma operator error. The source basis is rounded
only for polynomial evaluation; its difference is paid by
||M(v-v0)||<=2||v-v0||. Therefore a full physical residual bound is

 rho_z=complete_model_residual + gamma_K ||w|| + 2||v-v0||.

The old low candidate precision remains 1024 bits. New polynomial squared
